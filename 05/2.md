在等腰 $\bigtriangleup {ABC}$ 中, ${AB} = {BC}$ . 在其内部选取一点 $M$ ,使得 $\angle {AMC} = \; 2\angle {ABC}$ . 在线段 ${AM}$ 上取一点 $K$ ，使得 $\angle {BKM} = \angle {ABC}$ . 证明: ${BK} = {MK} + {CM}$ .

证明

如图，延长 ${CM}$ 与 ${BK}$ 交于点 $L$ ，则 $\angle {KLM} = \angle {KMC} - \angle {LKM} = \angle {AMC} - \; \angle {BKM} = 2\angle {ABC} - \angle {BKM} = \angle {LKM}$ ，即 $\bigtriangleup  {MLK}$ 是等腰三角形，故 ${MK} = {ML}$ . 所以， ${MK} + {CM} = {ML} + {CM} = {CL}$ ，故只需证明 ${BK} = {CL}$ .

因为 $\angle {BAK} = \angle {BKM} - \angle {ABK} = \angle {ABC} - \angle {ABK} = \angle {CBK}$ ， $\angle {ABK} = \; \angle {ABC} - \angle {KBC} = \angle {KLM} - \angle {KBC} = \angle {BCL}$ ，结合 ${AB} = {BC}$ ，知 $\bigtriangleup  {ABK} \cong \; \bigtriangleup  {BCL}$ . 所以 ${BK} = {CL}$ ,命题得证.
