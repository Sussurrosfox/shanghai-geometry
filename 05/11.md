已知 $\bigtriangleup {ABC}$ 中, $\angle C = {90}^{ \circ  }, D$ 为 ${AC}$ 上一点, $K$ 为 ${BD}$ 上一点,且 $\angle {ABC} = \; \angle {KAD} = \angle {AKD}$ . 证明: ${BK} = {2DC}$ .

证明

如图,在 ${BD}$ 的延长线上取点 $E$ ,使得 ${KD} = {DE}$ ,结合题设条件,有 $\bigtriangleup {KAE}$ 是直角三角形,又 $\angle {EKA} = \angle {ABC}$ ,则 $\bigtriangleup {AKE} \backsim  \bigtriangleup {CBA}$ ,因此

$$
\frac{AE}{KE} = \frac{CA}{BA},
$$

①

$\angle {BAD} = \angle {BEA}$ . 又 $\angle {DBA} = \angle {EBA}$ ,所以 $\bigtriangleup {ABD} \backsim  \bigtriangleup {EBA}$ ,则

$$
\frac{AB}{AD} = \frac{EB}{EA}.
$$

②

将①，②两式相乘，得 $\frac{AC}{AD} = \frac{BE}{KE}$ ，所以 ${BE} = {2AC}$ ，因此

$$
{BK} = {BE} - {KE} = {2AC} - {2AD} = {2CD}.
$$

原问题得证.
